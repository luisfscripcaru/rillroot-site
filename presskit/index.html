About the game

Short: Rillroot is a cozy logic puzzle for Android. Lead a root from every thirsty plant to a pond, cover every tile, and never let two roots cross.

Longer: Every board is a little garden of chubby plants and stone ponds. Drag a root from a plant to the water and it grows behind your finger. The rules fit in one breath, but the short way is often the trap: a root that takes the nearest pond can leave a corner of soil nobody can reach. Every level has exactly one answer, there's no timer and no lives, and you can pull a root back up whenever you change your mind.

As you play, new plants join the garden with rules of their own, and they mix on the same boards. There's a new daily puzzle every day and a daily race against other players.

How it plays

  • Drag a root from a plant to a pond. Each pond feeds one root.
  • Roots never cross.
  • Cover every tile to finish the board.

New plants, new rules

  • Bamboo roots turn exactly once.
  • The Lotus needs two roots, each to its own pond.
  • Mushroom pairs share one thread.
  • Logs let roots pass straight through, even across each other.

Features

  • Hundreds of hand-checked levels, from a tiny 4×5 garden to Extreme 7×11 boards, with more added over time.
  • A daily challenge, easy on Monday and hard by Sunday.
  • A daily race, plus weekly and all-time leaderboards.
  • Daily and weekly quests.
  • Three helpers for when you're stuck: Grow, Weeder and Rain.
  • A colour-blind option that marks every plant with its own flower.

Launch gift: everyone who pre-registers on Google Play gets the First Bloom frame when the game comes out.
