SHORT
Rillroot is a cozy logic puzzle for Android. Lead a root from every thirsty plant to a pond, cover every tile, and never let two roots cross.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Drag a root from a plant to a pond. Each pond feeds one root.
- Roots never cross.
- Cover every tile to finish the board.

NEW PLANTS, NEW RULES
- Bamboo roots turn exactly once.
- The Lotus needs two roots, each to its own pond.
- Mushroom pairs share one thread.
- Logs let roots pass straight through, even across each other.
